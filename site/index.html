The problem

When housing becomes an asset class, people become collateral.

Investors outbid families. Platforms favor the fastest click. Middlemen charge for access to information that should be public. We think that's backwards.

Shelter first

A stable home is the foundation for work, health, and community. It shouldn't depend on market timing or who has the better scraper.

No centralized gatekeepers

Alert services that scrape for everyone share one IP pool — and pass the cost to you. We run the search on your machine instead.

People over profit

We're not a fund, a broker, or a landlord. easyHouse is a tool for renters — open, local, and focused on getting you housed.

How it works

Your computer watches. You get the alert.

  1. 1
    Install the agent

    One app on your Mac, Windows, or Linux. No cloud account required to scrape.

  2. 2
    Set your search area

    Pick a city and radius. We geocode listings and filter by distance from center.

  3. 3
    Get notified locally

    Email alerts when something new appears in your zone — deduped across platforms.

What we believe

Homes exist so people can live in them.

  • Renting is not a side hustle for your landlord's portfolio
  • Listing data shouldn't be locked behind subscription walls
  • Your search activity belongs to you, not a platform's server farm
  • Speed matters — but fairness matters more
